OutTheDoor: What to Wear

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 25 October 2026

OutTheDoor ("the app", "we", "us") is made in Sydney, Australia. This policy explains what information the app uses and how. The short version: we don't collect, store or sell your personal information.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location while you are using it, so it can show the weather for where you are. Your location is used only to request a weather forecast. We do not store it, build a history of it, or link it to you.

You can turn location access off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > OutTheDoor.

Weather data

Forecasts are provided by Apple Weather (Apple WeatherKit). To get a forecast, the app sends your approximate coordinates to a weather service, which returns the forecast. Apple's handling of data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share personal information with third parties.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
