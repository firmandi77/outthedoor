Frequently asked questions

The app doesn't show weather for my location.

Check that Location access is turned on for OutTheDoor: open Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > OutTheDoor and choose "While Using the App". You also need an internet connection to load the forecast.

The forecast looks different from another weather app.

Weather providers use different models, so forecasts can vary slightly, especially for rain chances. OutTheDoor uses Apple Weather data.

Do I need an account?

No. OutTheDoor doesn't require an account or sign-in.
